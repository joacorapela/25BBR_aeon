

\subsubsection*{Module 1 – Contributions to the generation of new ideas, tools, methodologies or knowledge}

\begin{instruction}

Explain your contributions to the generation of new ideas, tools,
methodologies or knowledge. For example, you could include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item  how you communicated your ideas and results
    \item  key outputs, such as data sets, software, research and policy publications
    \item  skills acquired from past research projects
\end{itemize}

\end{instruction}

\subsubsection*{Module 2 – The development of others and maintenance of effective working relationships}

\begin{instruction}
Explain your contributions to the development of others and maintenance of
effective working relationships. For example, you could include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item expertise you provided that was critical to the team’s success
    \item teaching activities, workshops and mentoring
    \item your leadership in shaping the direction of a team
\end{itemize}

\end{instruction}

\subsubsection*{Module 3 – Contributions to the wider research and innovation community}

\begin{instruction}
Explain your contributions to the wider research and innovation community.
Examples of what you could include are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item positions of responsibility
    \item commitments such as reviewing, editing and committee work
    \item your strategic leadership in influencing a research agenda
\end{itemize}
\end{instruction}

\subsubsection*{Module 4 – Contributions to broader research/innovation-users and audiences and towards wider societal benefit}

\begin{instruction}

Explain your contributions to broader research or innovation users and
audiences and towards wider societal benefit. Examples of what you could
include are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item  how you've engaged with the public sector, clients and the wider public
    \item  research which has contributed to public understanding
    \item  research which has contributed to policy development
\end{itemize}

\end{instruction}
